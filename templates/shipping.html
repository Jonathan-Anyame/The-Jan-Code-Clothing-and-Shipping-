Shipping Information

Insured delivery worldwide. Choose sea or air shipping for goods you source with us.

Worldwide Shipping

We ship to virtually every country in the world. We partner with trusted international carriers to ensure your package arrives safely and on time.

Packaging & Shipping Options

  • Sea Shipping — charged by volume (CBM)
    $260 per CBM · 30–45 days · best for bulk orders and lower cost
  • Air Shipping — charged by weight (kg)
    $19 per kg · 7–14 days · faster delivery when you need it sooner

Transit times are estimates from dispatch and can vary by destination and customs. Final quotes depend on total CBM (sea) or total kg (air).

Order Tracking

All orders include a unique tracking number. You can track your package in real time from dispatch to delivery.

Package Forwarding

We consolidate multiple purchases and ship them together to save you money on shipping costs.

Shipping Protection

All packages are insured and tracked. In case of loss or damage, we'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue.
